Western US Climate and Snow Drought Explorer

Snow, water, carbon, and vegetation observations across the Western US, 1990–2026. Each chart and map reads directly from the underlying data.

Map Interactive Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF maps — climatology, raw value, or anomaly, by month/season/year. Time Series Monthly raw value or standardized anomaly, full record, one product at a time. Seasonal Cycle Water-year climatology plus highlight years, one product at a time. SiB4 Modeled biosphere drought response by plant functional type and time of day. Summary Table Every product, one month/season/year, any region, at a glance. Heatmap Standardized anomaly by product and time, one matrix per category. Classification Dry / warm-and-dry / warm snow drought by winter, 1991–2026. Figure Gallery Browse and download pre-rendered static maps and heatmaps. About Methodology, data sources, and references.
