Regional summary vs. fixed calendar-month climatology

Computed on demand from the same mean-centered anomaly values as Time Series. Color = physical stress direction, not raw sign: stress (drier/hotter/less productive) vs. relief. Native indices (SPI/SPEI/EDDI/PDSI/ForDRI/ESI) are already a fixed-timescale statistic (e.g. SPI-03 = trailing 3 months), so a season window shows the reading "as of <month>" instead of re-aggregating it. Percent-of-normal is omitted for native indices and near-zero baselines (unstable).
