SNOTEL Snowpack

The NRCS SNOTEL network — 800+ automated stations measuring mountain snow water equivalent (SWE). Each station is colored by its SWE relative to the 1990–2024 median for the selected day — scrub or play through the snow season, and click a station for its own daily record. Percent of median is only meaningful in the snow season. The current water year's data is provisional (NRCS).

Click a station for its snowpack record

Snow water equivalent versus station elevation for the selected day, in the metric chosen above (% of median, percentile, or raw mm). Percent-of-median is a ratio that can run to several hundred percent where the median snowpack is tiny, so that view is capped at 200% (off-scale points shown as triangles); the raw-mm and percentile views are not affected by that.
