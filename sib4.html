SiB4 — Modeled Biosphere Drought Response

SiB4 (the Simple Biosphere model, version 4; Colorado State University) is a process-based land biosphere model that simulates photosynthesis, respiration, and plant water stress from weather and soil moisture. Its modeled fluxes and stress functions are shown here by plant functional type (PFT) and time of day.

Land cover is fixed in time; bare ground carries no carbon flux.

SiB4's three stress functions (rstfac) that scale photosynthesis — leaf-surface humidity (dryness of the air at the leaf), root-zone soil water, and temperature. Each runs 0–1 where 1 = unstressed and lower = more limiting.

Local solar time (hour 12 ≈ local noon). Model diagnostic, not observation.

Standardized anomaly (σ); positive = stress direction. Check a dataset to add it.
