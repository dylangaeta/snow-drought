SiB4 — Modeled Biosphere Drought Response

Modeled Western US biosphere response resolved by plant functional type (PFT) and time of day.

Land cover is fixed in time; bare ground carries no carbon flux.

Stress intensity = 1 − factor; higher is more limiting.

Local solar time (hour 12 ≈ local noon). Model diagnostic, not observation.

Standardized anomaly (σ); positive = stress direction. Check a dataset to add it.

Source: snowdrought-carbon (private research codebase, NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory · Cooperative Institute for Research in Environmental Sciences).
