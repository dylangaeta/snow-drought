Anomaly heatmaps by product and time

Standardized anomaly (σ) per product: one row per product/response, one column per month or year. Each row is sign-flipped to its own variable's stress direction (e.g. low snowpack is the stress direction, not high), so gold is always stress and teal is always relief, regardless of the variable's raw sign convention.
