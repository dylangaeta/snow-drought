Snow drought classification by winter type

Dierauer, Allen & Whitfield (2019, Water Resources Research) classification. A winter is a snow drought when its annual peak SWE falls below the climatological mean peak SWE (this flags more than half of winters, as in the paper). Each snow-drought winter is then typed by its Oct–Apr drivers, keyed on precipitation: warm (precipitation at or above normal), dry (precipitation deficit without above-normal warmth), or warm & dry (precipitation deficit with above-normal temperature). One point per winter (1991–2026); by default x = temperature, y = peak SWE and color = precipitation, with the classification in each point's hover text.
